# P3.20 Amendment 1 — Exact statistics and canonical rank gate

Committed before runner implementation and before any registered lineage/probe executes.

## Multivariate geometry statistic

For each paired lineage difference on the four primary geometry components, compute component sample standard deviations across lineages. Components with zero standard deviation contribute zero.

For component j:

z_j = mean(d_j) / sd(d_j)

and

T = sum_j z_j^2.

The null is generated by 50,000 deterministic paired sign flips using seed 3217000, applying one sign per lineage to the entire four-component vector to preserve within-lineage covariance.

Upper-tail empirical p = (1 + null T >= observed T)/(1 + draws).

Individual components use two-sided paired sign-flip tests on absolute mean difference with seeds 3217100 + component index and Holm correction across the four components.

The half-A/half-B direction requirement uses the sign of the unstandardized mean paired difference for the Holm-surviving component.

## Standardized Euclidean geometry distance

Within each probe half and each of the four components, scale by the pooled sample standard deviation of FULL_OWN and BLANK lineage values. A component with pooled SD <= 1e-12 is omitted.

For each lineage, calculate Euclidean distance from DONOR_PFM to FULL_OWN and from DONOR_PFM to BLANK in this standardized space.

The transfer test statistic is the paired lineage difference:

distance(DONOR, BLANK) - distance(DONOR, FULL).

A positive mean is tested by one-sided paired sign flips with 50,000 draws, seeds 3217200 for half A and 3217201 for half B.

## Addressability ties and canonical efficacy

For each probe/motif prevalence calculation, a lineage contributes presence at most once per motif. Ties in specificity are resolved by higher probe prevalence, then lexical motif order.

The registration phrase “higher than at least 23 of 24” is tightened to remove ambiguity with the empirical rank formula:

**P3.20 calls the canonical Numogram address-privileged only if its addressability score is strictly greater than all 24 rewired-control scores.**

This gives the preregistered attainable empirical rank p = 1/25 = 0.04.

No other addressability statistic rescues failure of this gate.
