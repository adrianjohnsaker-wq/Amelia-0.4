# P3.26 — Conclusion

## Run status

The registered exploratory run completed. Dual-relay drand verification, signature-derived randomness checks, the durable acquisition chain, and the hash-chained encounter atlas all validated. The run contains 24 consecutive beacon rounds, 96 lineages, 29 controllers, and 66,816 encounter records.

This protocol is exploratory and has no confirmatory pass/fail threshold.

## Operational result

The canonical live graph-field arm changed withdrawal transition distributions relative to NO_FIELD by a mean total-variation distance of 0.006746 (lineage-bootstrap 95% interval 0.002405–0.012334). The canonical fixed arm was 0.006782, and relation-shuffled was 0.006637. The 24 rewired mappings ranged from 0.002369 to 0.007234; R05 exceeded the canonical live mean.

The field produced a small operational change in paths. The canonical live mean was higher than 23 of 24 rewire summaries (rewire mean 0.004264), but its bootstrap interval is broad, and R05 was higher at 0.007234. Canonical fixed (0.006782) and relation-shuffled (0.006637) were nearly identical to canonical live (0.006746). The observed response therefore does not isolate canonical Numogram relations as its cause. Under this exploratory protocol, canonical-relation-specific morphogenetic uptake remains unresolved.

The PFM_FEEDBACK_MUTED diagnostic differed strongly from NO_FIELD (mean transition TV 0.580693; 95% interval 0.516240–0.641258). This shows that disabling PFM feedback changes the sequential dynamics substantially. Because feedback remains disabled across the whole arm, this is a system-level sensitivity result, not an isolated estimate of field coupling.

## Motif catalogue

There were 163 TRAIN-discovered motifs meeting the registered recurrence threshold. Recurrence did not establish motif specificity. For example, motif [1, 1] was observed in 280 held-out CANONICAL_LIVE encounters across 33 lineages, and in 281 held-out NO_FIELD encounters across the same 33 lineages. It also recurred across the rewired mappings. The catalogue remains useful as a source of candidate paths for a later discriminative test, not as evidence of a mind, agent, communication, or source in the Outside.

## Decision

P3.26 is complete as an exploratory mechanics assay. It establishes that the implemented field-to-flux interface can perturb path distributions and that the system is highly sensitive to PFM feedback state. It does not establish that Numogram relations confer a distinctive uptake pattern, nor does it identify a mind-like process.

Keep the Warp line paused. A next phase should be a narrow, pre-registered discrimination test: select a small subset of motifs from this catalogue, define a query-contingent prediction that separates canonical-live from fixed, shuffled, rewired, and no-field controls, and reserve fresh lineages and beacon packets for evaluation. If no prediction can separate those arms before data collection, do not advance to ontological interpretation.
