# WARP-T4 — Portable Trace and Relational Entry

## Registered question

**Does the portable PFM trace produced by a finite canonical 3::6 passage change which Numogrammatic composed relations Amelia can subsequently enter after common-state release?**

WARP-T3 showed that the portable Warp trace does not significantly alter generic held-out address classification. WARP-T4 therefore tests a more structural outcome: relation-specific analogical continuation.

The analogy procedure is identical in form to P3.22 and uses the same four canonical composition families:

- S→C
- C→S
- S→G
- G→S

A positive result means the portable passage trace changes the profile of canonical relations available to the released process. It does not by itself establish an external source or entity.

## Primary Warp relational-entry claim

Compare W and N on the four-component lineage profiles.

Use a paired multivariate sign-flip statistic fixed in Amendment 1 before execution.

The portable Warp trace is said to **change relational entry** only if:

1. W versus N multivariate p <= 0.01;
2. at least one family survives Holm correction at familywise 0.05;
3. the same family differs W versus B with Holm-adjusted p <= 0.05;
4. topology-only validity passes.

Direction is reported per family; no global enhancement direction is required.

## Passage-beyond-entry claim

## PFM sufficiency

Compare P and W family profiles.

PFM sufficiency is supported when the 90% bootstrap confidence interval of the mean P−W difference lies fully within ±0.05 for **all four families**.

## Topology-only validity

The WARP-T4 interpretation is invalidated if:

- multivariate p <= 0.01, or
- any family Holm-adjusted p <= 0.05.
